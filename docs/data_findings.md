## 4. Time to activate, status chasing, churn and NPS

### Stage durations (days)

Every stage is slow: a contacted lead waits ~3 days for a needs visit, then

## 7. Which segments to prioritise, and why

**1. Professional offices first** (clinics, CA firms, coaching centres, law and real-estate offices).
- Largest number of activations (138) and the best activation rate among sizeable segments (11.1%).
- Lowest actual CAC per activation (Rs 10,695) and 1.6-month revenue payback on a Rs 6,796/month bundle.
- Standard 12-month contracts, 14% churn, 86.1% have a GSTIN so KYC is straightforward.
- Needs are uniform (fibre + cloud email + a few postpaid lines + backup/security) and need no site survey, so the digital advisor can close them end to end.

**2. Small multi-site businesses second.**
- Highest value: Rs 31,233/month average, 57% of all recurring value from 23% of activations; payback 0.5 months.
- Lowest churn with field sales and inbound; but the bundle (SD-WAN, failover, CCTV per site) has dependencies and 24-month contracts, so the advisor should quote and then hand off to a partner for the multi-site install.

**3. Micro retail and services only through the self-serve channel.**
- 54.2% of leads but just 4.1% activate; actual CAC Rs 20,007 against a Rs 2,505 bundle (8.0 months payback, before margin).
- The field model cannot serve this segment profitably; the digital journey is the *only* way to make it work, and it also solves the two micro-specific drops: "Only wanted a mobile plan" and the no-GSTIN dead end (47.2% of micro leads have no GSTIN).
- Watch churn: micro customers from digital sources churn at 38.5% vs 11.1% from field sales (small samples), so the tracker and a right-sized starter bundle matter here.

**4. Small manufacturers: keep field-assisted.** Only 289 leads (5.8%), slowest activation
(20.5 days) because of leased-line installs, and the worst NPS (-46).
Good economics (0.8-month payback) but needs a site survey; use the advisor for discovery and quote, then hand off.

## 8. The product catalogue, rule by rule

25 products in 5 categories. Prices are per unit per month with a min-max range
(the engine will quote the minimum as the list price and keep the range for partner discretion).
Setup fees are one-time per unit. Contract length of a bundle = the longest minimum contract among its products
(this is how every activated bundle in the data was priced).

### Rules in plain English

1. **City tier.** Every product is sold in tier 1 and 2 except P03 Biz Fibre 1G, which is tier-1 only.
   A tier-2 customer asking for 1 Gbps fibre must be offered Biz Fibre 300 (P02) or a leased line instead.
2. **Minimum size.** P03 Biz Fibre 1G: 5+ employees; P05 Internet Leased Line 20: 10+ employees; P06 Internet Leased Line 100: 25+ employees; P10 Managed SD-WAN: 10+ employees, 3+ sites; P12 Cloud Telephony / SIP: 3+ employees; P21 Managed Firewall: 10+ employees; P24 Machine Monitoring IoT: 10+ employees.
   Smaller businesses get the next product down (e.g. Biz Fibre 300 instead of 1G, Biz Fibre 300 + failover instead of a leased line).
3. **GSTIN required** for 10 products: P03, P05, P06, P10, P12, P13, P14, P15, P21, P24. These are the regulated or high-value
   items (leased lines, 1G fibre, SD-WAN, telephony, toll-free, SMS, WhatsApp API, firewall, machine IoT). A business with only a
   Udyam number can still buy the other 15 products, which cover the entire micro bundle
   (fibre or AirFiber, postpaid lines, POS, website, CCTV, Wi-Fi, email, backup, endpoint security).
4. **Dependencies (requires one of).** 7 products only work on top of a connectivity product in the same bundle:
   - P09 Backup 4G/5G Failover needs P01 or P02 or P03 or P05 or P06
   - P10 Managed SD-WAN needs P01 or P02 or P03 or P05 or P06
   - P11 Static IP needs P01 or P02 or P03
   - P12 Cloud Telephony / SIP needs P01 or P02 or P03 or P04 or P05 or P06
   - P21 Managed Firewall needs P01 or P02 or P03 or P05 or P06
   - P22 Secure Wi-Fi with Guest Network needs P01 or P02 or P03 or P04
   - P23 Cloud CCTV needs P01 or P02 or P03 or P04 or P05 or P06
   The engine adds the cheapest eligible base link automatically and explains why, or removes the dependent product if no base link is eligible.
5. **Per-unit quantities.** Products are priced per site, per line, per user, per device, per camera, per counter or per vehicle; the engine
   sizes quantities from the profile (sites, employees, counters, cameras, vehicles).
6. **Install days** drive the expected activation date on the tracker; the bundle's expected activation is the longest install among its products.
